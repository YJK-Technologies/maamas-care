Frequently Asked Questions

Everything prospective residents and parents ask before booking a room at Maama's Care Women Hostel.

Ask Our Warden Directly

We collect a flat refundable security deposit of ₹10,000 for all room categories (Single, Double, and Triple Sharing). Upon completing a standard 30-day vacating notice, 100% of the deposit is refunded via UPI or bank transfer within 5 working days after room handover—with zero arbitrary maintenance deductions.

The minimum lock-in period is 2 months, followed by a simple 30-day written notice via email or our WhatsApp support desk (78248 09809) before vacating. Short-term internships (30–60 days) are also accommodated subject to room availability.

Standard gate closure is 10:30 PM. However, IT and healthcare professionals working late or rotational shifts enjoy 24/7 biometric access after submitting their company ID and shift schedule to the warden.

Male visitors (including fathers and brothers) are welcome in the ground-floor reception lounge between 9:00 AM and 7:30 PM, and are strictly not permitted on residential floors. Mothers and sisters may stay overnight (subject to roommate consent) for up to 3 nights per month at a nominal ₹400/day meal & linen charge.

All common-area power, lighting, fans, Wi-Fi, and geyser usage are included in your rent. Only split AC consumption is billed on actuals using individual digital sub-meters (split equally among roommates) at standard TNEB slab rates.
